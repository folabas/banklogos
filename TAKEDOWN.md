# Takedown requests

If you represent a brand included here and want its logo removed or updated, we will act within **7 days**.

1. Open an issue using the **Takedown request** template, or email the maintainer (address in the npm package metadata).
2. Name the entity (id or brand name) and say whether you want it **removed** or **replaced** with a supplied file.
3. We may ask you to confirm you represent the brand, for example by replying from a company email domain.

After a removal:

- The logo files are deleted and the next release ships without them.
- The id stays reserved and is never reused. `getLogo()` returns `undefined` for it.
- The removal is listed in the changelog.
